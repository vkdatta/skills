Navigation System

Simple.
Clean. Flat.

Four navigation bars live at the edges of your workspace — top, right, bottom, and left. Each one is independent and always stays fully expanded, showing its navigation icons directly in the bar.

Open any bar's department browser to manage its visibility, choose which departments appear, reorder them by dragging, and toggle individual items on or off. Every bar remembers its own selection.

Tip — use the bar controls at the top of the department browser to show or hide any navigation bar.
